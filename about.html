What is UNICO?

UNICO is an acronym for “University & College Students.” This ministry is dedicated to serving students under PMM SIBLCC, with a focus on nurturing their spiritual journey. Through this ministry, students are encouraged to grow in faith, bear fruit, and multiply in their spiritual lives.

Three things we share together

  1. 01

    “You are part of the family in Kota Kinabalu!”

  2. 02

    “You will thrive, bear fruit, and multiply!”

  3. 03

    “You no longer live for yourself; Christ lives in you.”
